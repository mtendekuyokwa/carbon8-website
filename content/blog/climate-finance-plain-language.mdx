Carbon8 is a community-first climate organisation in Malawi, currently at **concept stage**.

## Why plain language matters

Climate finance is full of jargon. We are developing explainers that start from community questions, not donor templates.

## What we're exploring

- Community-led project ideas
- Transparent, small-scale funding flows
- GeoAI only as a support tool, with human review

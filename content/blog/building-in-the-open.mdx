This blog is where we will share **learning notes** as Carbon8 grows.

## What to expect

- Short, honest updates
- No invented impact numbers
- Community voices first

## Article system

Posts are written in MDX under `content/blog/`. Each file carries frontmatter (`title`, `description`, `date`, `tags`) and renders through the `/blog/:slug` route.

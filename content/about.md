# About Us

## Our Story

Carbon8 started with a simple observation: Malawi has no shortage of land that could be reforested, communities ready to do the work, and climate finance looking for credible projects to fund — but very little that connects the three. Environmental restoration projects often stall because they're designed far from the communities who have to live with them, or because they never make it far enough to meet the standards climate finance requires.

Carbon8 exists to close that gap. We're a Malawi-based social enterprise building afforestation, reforestation, and other environmental restoration projects from the ground up, with the communities who will carry them forward — and with an eye, from day one, on making those projects credible enough to eventually connect to carbon markets and climate finance.

We're early. There are no finished projects to point to yet, and we'd rather be honest about that than pretend otherwise. What we do have is a clear approach, a set of values we're building the company around, and a plan for how the first projects will take shape.

## Our Approach

We treat each project as a long-term partnership with a community, not a transaction.

- **Start with the community, not the site.** Before any planting happens, we spend time listening — to chiefs, cooperatives, farmer groups, and district offices — about what the land means to them and what they need from a project like this.
- **Design for the local benefit first.** Every project is built to create social and economic value for the community involved — employment, shared decision-making, and long-term benefit — alongside its environmental outcome.
- **Build toward verifiable standards.** From the outset, we design projects with the documentation and rigor that climate finance and carbon markets require, so credible projects don't stall at the funding stage.
- **Grow deliberately.** We'd rather develop a small number of projects properly than move quickly and lose community trust or financial credibility along the way.

## Our Values

- **Community-first.** Communities are partners in every project, not beneficiaries of a plan made without them.
- **Transparency.** We say plainly where a project stands — including now, when Carbon8 itself is just getting started.
- **Long-term commitment.** Restoration takes years, not a single planting season. We design and fund projects with that timeline in mind.
- **Rigor.** Credibility with funders and climate-finance partners comes from doing the technical and administrative work properly, not just the planting.
